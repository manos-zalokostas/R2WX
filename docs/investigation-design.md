# Investigation Design

## Investigation Design

The preceding examination was not produced through a single analytical pass.

Its development passed through two different investigative levels and eventually used three deliberately distinct boundaries of responsibility: analysis, epistemic discipline and author consciousness.

The boundaries were permeable. Every participant could reason across them, object to a finding, introduce contradictory material, or request that an apparently settled result be examined again.

Their primary responsibilities nevertheless remained distinct.

## Analysis — Gemini

Gemini held the principal responsibility for excavating the artifact.

Its investigation was conducted through deliberately constrained, largely faceless repository-analysis prompts rather than through participation in the ongoing interpretive discussion. It was required to inspect implementation material, follow relationships across the repository, retrieve supporting evidence and develop candidate explanations from what could actually be found.

Its responsibility was not to preserve an existing interpretation of R2WX.

It was to keep returning to the artifact.

## Epistemic discipline — ChatGPT

ChatGPT held the principal responsibility for what the accumulated evidence permitted the investigation to conclude.

Implementation observations, author testimony and attractive interpretations were not treated as equivalent forms of evidence. Larger conclusions could be resisted when their evidential bridge remained incomplete, classifications could be narrowed when their terminology exceeded what had been established, and previously accepted explanations could be reopened when additional evidence changed their meaning.

Its responsibility was not to maximize the significance of the findings.

It was to prevent the claims from outrunning what had been established.

## Consciousness — the author

The author's privileged position created a different responsibility.

Having constructed the artifact, the author could recognize when an explanation was locally correct while substantial engineering embodied elsewhere in the system remained unaccounted for.

That knowledge did not determine what the missing material would eventually be called or what larger conclusion it would support. It could instead require the investigation to continue: inspect further, connect apparently unrelated behavior, recover mechanisms that had not yet entered the reconstruction, and avoid treating a plausible partial explanation as completion.

The author's primary responsibility was therefore to prevent the investigation from closing while engineering embodied in the artifact remained undiscovered.

## Re-examination across the boundaries

These responsibilities did not establish three isolated analytical territories.

Gemini could propose interpretations. ChatGPT could perform implementation analysis. The author could challenge classifications. Each participant could contribute within another participant's primary jurisdiction.

Likewise, every participant could object.

An objection did not determine the replacement conclusion.

It reopened the investigation.

Additional repository evidence could require the author to reconsider an earlier reconstruction. An epistemic objection could return a candidate interpretation to repository analysis. Author awareness of unexplored implementation could require both analytical positions to regenerate their explanations.

Re-examination therefore carried no predetermined direction.

A finding could become stronger.

It could become narrower.

Two apparently related findings could separate.

Apparently unrelated mechanisms could become connected.

A classification could change.

Or a claim could be rejected.

The responsibility boundaries determined what each participant was principally accountable for. They did not protect any participant's previous analysis from revision.

## Two investigative levels

The triad was not the beginning of the investigation.

An earlier analytical level established the factual basis from which the later reconstruction could proceed.

## Level 1 — Establishing the basis

The initial work combined author interrogation with original implementation artifacts.

This produced a series of reports in which author knowledge could be compared against concrete implementation evidence rather than accepted as sufficient by itself.

Those reports established substantial technical and historical ground.

They did not, however, constitute a complete reconstruction of R2WX as one engineering object. Their unit of progress was principally the individual claim, artifact or question being investigated.

## Level 2 — Reconstructing the whole

The second level deliberately reopened the object.

A fresh analytical context was introduced. Gemini performed blind repository excavation. ChatGPT operated from a resistant epistemic position. The author moved from answering individual implementation questions toward requiring the investigation to account for the artifact as a whole.

Earlier findings remained available, but they were not immune to re-examination.

The objective was no longer merely to establish whether individual claims were supported.

It was to determine how the observed decisions related to one another across the complete system.

The eight pressure points used in this paper were not supplied as that investigation's original analytical framework.

They are part of its result.

## Publication and repository exposure

A fourth participant, GitHub Copilot, entered temporarily under a narrower jurisdiction after the artifact and its engineering interpretation had substantially stabilized.

Its role concerned GitHub reality: repository presentation, discoverability, terminology, topic selection and indexing.

That work did not determine what R2WX was allowed to mean.

The engineering investigation determined the claims that could legitimately accompany the artifact; the GitHub-facing pass addressed how those claims and the repository could be exposed effectively within that environment.

## Inspecting the investigation

The methodology described here is not offered as proof that the resulting interpretation is correct.

It records how the interpretation was produced.

Representative prompts, evidence classifications and selected instances in which findings were reopened, narrowed, rejected or reorganized can accompany this section so that the analytical process is itself available for inspection.

The same distinction used throughout the R2WX examination applies here:

the procedure can be exposed; its value remains for the reader to judge.
